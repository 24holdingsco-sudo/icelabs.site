Whisky is a crime drama that explores the world of whiskey smuggling and the dark side of the spirits industry. Set in Scotland, the series follows a family caught between legitimate business and the criminal networks that control the black market for aged whiskey.

The series excels at depicting the culture of whiskey — the craftsmanship, the history, and the obsession that drives collectors and criminals alike. The details of whiskey production, aging, and valuation are woven into the plot, giving the show a distinctive texture that sets it apart from other crime dramas.

The performances are strong, with the family dynamics providing the emotional core. The tension between preserving tradition and adapting to modern realities drives both the business and personal storylines. The Scottish setting is beautifully captured, with the Highlands providing a dramatic backdrop.

The criminal underworld of whiskey smuggling is a fresh premise for a crime drama, and the series makes the most of it. The heists, double-crosses, and power struggles are grounded in the specifics of the whiskey trade, giving them a authenticity that generic crime stories lack.

Whisky is essential for fans of crime dramas looking for something different. It's a dram of fine single malt in a sea of generic beer.
